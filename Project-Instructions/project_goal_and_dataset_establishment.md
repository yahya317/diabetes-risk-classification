## Group Assignment

**General Description:** Submit to Canvas a single-page PDF containing one narrative paragraph motivating your project, followed by a clear project goal and a brief description of the dataset you intend to use. In addition, create the GitHub repository for your project. For this task, you will be required to create and upload the reporting set (20% of the original dataset), which must remain UNTOUCHED until the final stage of the project.

**Why are we doing this?** Before embarking on a project, you must identify a motivation for your work. Why is this worth doing? The project goal serves as a lens that focuses this motivation into a clear direction. When someone reads your goal and justification, they should immediately understand what you aim to achieve and why it matters. The process of defining the goal forces you to distill your thoughts and identify something meaningful to pursue. Moreover, creating a reporting set early and keeping it untouched ensures that your final results remain unbiased.

**What will we do?** As a group, you should have a discussion of topics and then make a decision. This topic should be of interest to all. Then, everyone in the group will explore the topic (aka research). You will discuss what you found individually, and the team should come to a consensus on the direction to follow. At this point, you will develop a question or objective, a justification/motivation based on existing discussions in the area (this is where your references come in). The group will create the reporting set and upload it to the GitHub previously created for the project, and draft the PDF for submission.

### Tips for success

- **Follow your curiosity.** Discovering that an idea leads nowhere is part of the process and just as valuable as finding a promising direction.
- **Read widely.** Explore different references and use them for inspiration. This phase cannot be rushed.
- **Listen actively.** Pay attention to what your teammates say, and reflect on your own thoughts in the context of theirs.
- **Build shared understanding.** Ask clarifying questions, paraphrase ideas, and confirm your understanding. If your teammate disagrees with your rephrasing, keep refining until you both align.
- **Be mindful of your emotions.** Creative work is deeply influenced by your emotional state. Pay attention to how you feel while thinking or listening—those emotions can be powerful signals guiding your process.

**How will I know I have succeeded?** You will meet expectations on this assignment when you follow the criteria in the rubric below.

## Rubric

### Formatting

- One page (one-sided, no exceptions)
- PDF format
- Order:
  1. Title
  2. Group members
  3. Course and Date
  4. Motivation narrative
  5. Research Question
  6. Dataset Description
  7. GitHub repository
  8. References

### Motivation Narrative

- One (1) paragraph
- **Goal:** Justify why your project proposal is worthwhile and explain the topical context necessary to justify the research question.
- **Target:** "unfamiliar audience"
- Include relevant major ideas and cite references where they were established.

### Research Question

- One sentence
- **Goal:** Capture your project's motivation in a clear, focused question.

### Dataset Description

- One (1) paragraph
- **Goal:** Briefly describe the main characteristics of your dataset.
- Include:
  - Data source and download location (of the original data).
  - Sample size.
  - Number of features.
  - Basic demographic information, summarized by some basic statistic (e.g., mean and SD of participants' age, sex proportion, etc.)

### GitHub Repository

- Provide the repository link.
- This is the repository where you will upload all project deliverables (results and report) at the end of the course.
- **Important:** At this stage, your repository must include a `Data` folder containing the reporting set (20% of the dataset), which must remain untouched until the project's final phase. If this reporting set is too large for GitHub, upload it to a platform such as Google Drive and include a link with appropriate download permissions.

### References

- All references should be listed at the end of the document.
- Use IEEE Documentation style.
